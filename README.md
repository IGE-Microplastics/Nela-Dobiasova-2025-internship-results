Plastic pollution is a global issue impacting worldwide ecosystems and human and animal well-being and health. In order to effectively evaluate plastic waste management and global policies aimed at reducing microplastic (MP) pollution, it is essential to have a better understanding of how microplastics behave in the environment. In recent years, numerous protocols have been developed to quantify MP particles of different sizes in various ecosystems. This multiplicity of methods makes it difficult to compare and synthesize results from different studies. As part of my internship, I developed an approach based on cumulative particle counts that allows for the intercomparison of MP observational studies by extrapolating observed concentrations to any desired particle size range and estimating the associated uncertainty. The method was tested using both simulated and real datasets.
